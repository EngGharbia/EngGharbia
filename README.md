### Hi, I'm Abdurraouf 👋

I'm a software engineer from Tripoli, Libya, with over 10 years of experience building web and mobile apps. I currently lead the IT department at the Economic Information & Documentation Center (Ministry of Economy & Trade).

I mostly build systems for government and fintech: online government services, payments, digital wallets and identity verification. I work mainly with .NET and Flutter, and I usually build the whole thing, from the database to the app.

Most of my code is in private repositories because it belongs to clients. You can see my projects on **[bengharbia.ly](https://bengharbia.ly)**.

- **HuwiyaGate**: identity verification for Libyan organizations, using passport scanning and face matching.
